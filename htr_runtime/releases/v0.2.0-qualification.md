## What changed

TAU v2 handles eligible sparse-attention prefills, partition4 handles selected small MoE batches, and dense prefill expands its weights per call rather than retaining a multi-GB BF16 shadow. Sticky deterministic compilation is supplemented by source/ABI-bound reference reduction geometry: GDN XBLOCK=1 for MTP0 and XBLOCK=4 for MTP3, both with two warps; MTP3 also retains its reference RMS reduction at R0_BLOCK=16384 / 32 warps. The generated arithmetic and native static launch path are preserved; incompatible serialized geometry is rejected.

Compiled-cache namespaces are tied to runtime/version/source and serving levers. Compiler caches and new-shape JIT are allowed; persistent expanded16-bit weight shadows are not. These are different storage policies.

## Limits and deferred work

QSA is approximate sparse attention, not full-attention parity. Original equivalence roster is not broad semantic-quality qualification. Concurrent scheduling may change generated tokens. Image/video execution not qualified. Known short-prefill overhead and PLE/I/O tails are not claimed repaired. FP8 KV and additional optimization are deferred. The fixed1M profiles prove startup, public seed warmups through32768 and short requests, not full-million prefill or retrieval. Four slots share one27GiB KV pool, not four independent million-token histories. Ready-state scratch saving is not equivalent to peak-traffic memory saving; startup still used substantial swap. Predecessor speed and26-output equivalence evidence are explicitly predecessor-scoped; final-image lifetime parity is the18-pair diagnostic. First-use and new-shape compilation remain possible; no prebuilt cache archive is distributed.
